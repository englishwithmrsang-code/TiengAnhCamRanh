ESSAY 6.2: Flying a Model Plane vs. a Real Plane

Paragraph 1 (Introduction)

Have you ever wondered what it would be like to pilot a plane? Many people dream of doing this, but flying is an expensive hobby. However, people who fly model planes can appreciate the wonder of flight. Using remote-controlled model planes, they can enjoy the freedom of taking off in a plane and soaring through the sky. In fact, the experience of flying a model airplane is remarkably similar to piloting a real plane.

Paragraph 2 (Body 1: Pre-flight & Controls)

Even though people prepare their model airplanes for flight, they are aware that the procedures are the same as the pre-flight procedures that pilots follow. Both model planes and real planes require maintenance to be operated safely.

Paragraph 3 (Body 2: Physics)

Another similarity between the two airplanes is the physics involved in flying them. For example, just like a real airplane, a model plane is powered by an engine that creates forward power.

Paragraph 4 (Body 3: Steering Parts)

Both types of planes have similar parts as well. Both have a throttle that controls the speed of the plane, a rudder that steers, and ailerons on the wings.

Paragraph 5 (Conclusion)

The average person is surprised to find that there are so many similarities between model airplanes and real airplanes. Just like a real plane, a model airplane requires pre-flight procedures, operates on the same principles of physics, and has similar parts and systems.

Analysis Questions

1. What is the writer's purpose in this essay?

Đáp án:

The purpose of this essay is to compare flying a model plane with piloting a real plane and show how similar they are.

2. What are the points of comparison in this essay?

Đáp án:

The points of comparison are: (1) pre-flight procedures and maintenance, (2) physics of flying, and (3) control parts/systems (throttle, rudder, ailerons).

3. What method of organization does the writer use?

Đáp án:

Point-by-point method. (Mỗi đoạn thân bài so sánh một điểm chung giữa máy bay mô hình và máy bay thật).

4. Thesis Statement & Restatement in Conclusion?

Đáp án:

Thesis Statement: "In fact, the experience of flying a model airplane is remarkably similar to piloting a real plane."

Restated Thesis: "Just like a real plane, a model airplane requires pre-flight procedures, operates on the same principles of physics, and has similar parts and systems."
