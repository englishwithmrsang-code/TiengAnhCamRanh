🧠 Mindmap Recap: Tổng Hợp Các Chủ Đề & Ý Tưởng Đô Thị

📌 Nhấp chuột vào từng Branch (Nhánh) bên dưới để xem tóm tắt Nguyên nhân (Causes), Hậu quả (Effects), Giải pháp (Solutions) và Từ nối (Cohesive Devices) theo từng chủ đề!

🏙️ URBAN PROBLEMS & SOLUTIONS
🚗 1. TRAFFIC JAMS (Giao thông) ▼
⚠️ Causes & Effects
  • Too many private cars: Nhiều xe cá nhân
  • Limited public transport: Giao thông công cộng hạn chế
  • Narrow roads: Đường sá quá hẹp
  • Effects: Waste hours on the road, arrive late at work
💡 Solutions
  • Build more metro lines
  • Encourage travel by bus
🏭 2. AIR POLLUTION (Ô nhiễm) ▼
⚠️ Causes & Effects
  • Sources: Smoke from vehicles, factories & construction sites
  • Health risks: Coughing, breathing problems, eye infections
💡 Solutions
  • Limit old vehicles
  • Plant more trees along streets
  • Use electric bikes & public transport
❤️ 3. HEALTH & LIFESTYLE (Sức khỏe) ▼
⚠️ Causes & Effects
  • Work long hours, sit all day, eat fast food
  • Lack of exercise, sleep less than 6 hours
  • Effects: Feel stressed, tired, become overweight
💡 Solutions
  • Offer flexible working hours
  • Build more parks & cycling lanes
🌳 4. ENVIRONMENT & WASTE (Môi trường) ▼
⚠️ Causes & Effects
  • Huge amount of plastic waste
  • Dirty rivers/canals, plastic bags block drains
  • Causes flooding after heavy rain
💡 Solutions
  • Teach people to sort rubbish & recycle
  • Ban single-use plastic bags
🏙️ 5. CITY LIFE & HOUSING (Đời sống) ▼
⚠️ Causes & Effects
  • Mass migration for better jobs & schools
  • High living costs & overcrowding
  • Expensive rent, live in small, crowded flats
💡 Solutions
  • Build affordable housing
  • Create jobs in smaller towns
  • Limit night noise from construction
🔗 6. ESSENTIAL LINKING WORDS (Từ nối) ▼
📌 Cấu trúc nối bài quan trọng
  • Reason: This is mainly because / This is largely due to / Arise from the fact that
  • Result: As a result / As a consequence / Therefore
  • Solution: One effective solution is to / Should / Could
  • Conclusion: In conclusion / To sum up
