C. READING COMPREHENSION (Câu 26 - 30)

Passage: Life in the countryside is peaceful and full of beauty. People who live there enjoy the fresh air, green trees, and quiet surroundings. The environment is clean, and the pace of life is slow, which makes everyone feel relaxed and comfortable. Most people in the countryside are farmers. They grow rice, vegetables, and fruits or raise animals like chickens, cows, and buffaloes. Although their work is hard, they are very proud of it because it helps them earn a living and provide food for others.

Children in the countryside also have a happy and healthy life. They often play traditional games, ride bicycles, or go swimming in the river after school. There are not many cars or tall buildings, so it is safe for them to play outside. People here are friendly and always ready to help their neighbors.

I love the countryside because it brings me peace and happiness. Whenever I visit my grandparents there, I feel free, relaxed, and close to nature. Life in the countryside may be simple, but it is truly meaningful and beautiful.
